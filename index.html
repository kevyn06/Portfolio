SHEET 01 — WELCOME

Hey, I'm Kevyn.

Junior in Mechanical Engineering at Iowa State, first-gen college student, and someone who's happiest when a project involves both a spreadsheet and a pair of safety glasses. This site is my shop traveler — a running record of what I've built, where I've worked, and where I want to go next.

I'm most drawn to work that puts me in front of people — figuring out what they actually need and helping them get there. Sales engineering and manufacturing are where I'd love to land: sales for the people-facing problem-solving, manufacturing for the chance to work alongside the people actually running the floor and make their jobs easier. Design interests me too — genuinely, I think it's cool work — I just gravitate toward roles with less time behind a desk.

MAIN PROJECT — SOLIDWORKS DESIGN COURSE

Wind Turbine Build

Designed and built a small wind turbine from the ground up. Our first pass used gears machined out of wood — they didn't hold up under load. I helped redesign and switch the drivetrain to a 3D-printed gear, which solved the problem and got the turbine turning reliably. First real lesson in "the material you picked is the design flaw."

PLATINUM CRUSH

Cooler Root-Cause Diagnosis

Handed a clogged heat exchanger that cooled soybean oil with water. Instead of just clearing the clog, I traced the real problem upstream — the oil was being overheated before it ever reached the cooler. I proposed flushing the cooler with hot water through an existing bypass as a short-term fix. The team ended up solving it at the source by lowering the oil's heating temperature, which was the better fix — but getting to the right root cause first is the part I'm proud of.

V8 Teardown & Rebuild

Fully disassembled and rebuilt a V8 engine as part of an Engine Rebuild class I took at a community college while in high school. I still handle oil changes and brake jobs on my family's cars — plenty of the frustrating, hands-on troubleshooting from that class has come in handy since.
